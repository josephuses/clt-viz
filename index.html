The Central Limit Theorem predicts both the Center (Mean) and the Spread (Variance) of the sampling distribution.

Expected Mean:
\( \mu_{\bar{x}} = \mu \)
Expected Variance:
\( \sigma^2_{\bar{x}} = \frac{\sigma^2}{n} \)
Standard Deviation:
\( SD(\bar{x}) = \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} \)
